Keep the Realm Fair

The 11 Sanctum Rules

Select each rule to read its full details.

1. Respect Every Member

Treat players, staff, and visitors with basic respect. Harassment, bullying, discrimination, slurs, threats, targeted insults, and repeated unwanted behavior are not allowed.

  • Disagreements are fine; personal attacks are not.
  • Do not target someone because of who they are or how they play.
  • If someone asks you to stop a behavior that bothers them, stop.
2. No Griefing or Unauthorized Destruction

Do not damage, alter, destroy, or interfere with another player's builds, land, farms, storage, or property without clear permission.

  • This includes lava, water, fire, explosions, unwanted mobs, and indirect damage.
  • Do not remove blocks or decorations just because an area is not claimed.
  • Ask the owner before changing shared builds or community areas.
3. No Stealing or Unauthorized Access

Do not take items, open private storage, enter restricted areas, or use another player's resources without permission.

  • Accessible does not mean available to take.
  • Do not borrow items unless the owner agrees.
  • Return accidentally collected items and report theft to staff.
4. No Cheating, Hacking, or Exploiting

Play fairly. Hacked clients, X-ray, unauthorized combat advantages, duplication glitches, autoclickers, and unfair advantage tools are prohibited.

  • Only use modifications permitted by staff.
  • Do not abuse bugs or glitches for items, currency, or advantages.
  • Report exploits privately instead of using them.
5. Respect Land Claims and Building Boundaries

Respect protected land, claim boundaries, and agreements made with other players.

  • Do not bypass or interfere with another player's claim.
  • Ask before building close to someone else's property.
  • Do not intentionally block paths, entrances, farms, or access points.
6. Keep Chat and Voice Channels Appropriate

Keep conversations readable, relevant, and appropriate for the community.

  • No chat flooding, repeated messages, excessive caps, or deliberate disruption.
  • No malicious links, unsafe downloads, or inappropriate content.
  • Do not repeatedly advertise, solicit, or disrupt public chat or voice channels.
7. No Scamming or Trade Manipulation

All trades must be honest and follow the terms both players agreed to.

  • No fake payment confirmations, false promises, or misleading prices.
  • Do not take payment or items without providing the agreed return.
  • Keep trade agreements clear and report suspicious activity.
8. No Impersonation or False Staff Authority

Do not pretend to be another player, a staff member, or an official representative of The Crimson Sanctum.

  • Do not claim to have staff permissions you do not have.
  • Do not falsely announce punishments, events, or server decisions.
  • Staff will never need your account password or private login credentials.
9. Respect Staff Decisions and Use Reports Properly

Follow reasonable staff instructions while an issue is being handled. If you disagree, use the appropriate private appeal or report process.

  • Do not harass staff or disrupt chat over a moderation decision.
  • Provide honest information and evidence when submitting a report.
  • Do not knowingly submit false or malicious reports.
10. No Advertising or Unauthorized Promotion

Do not advertise other servers, communities, services, or social media accounts without staff permission.

  • This applies to chat, signs, books, item names, private messages, and disguised advertisements.
  • Do not repeatedly solicit members to leave the Sanctum.
  • Ask staff before arranging promotions or partnerships.
11. No Ban Evasion or Punishment Circumvention

Do not bypass a ban, mute, warning, or other restriction by using alternate accounts, another person's account, or another method of evasion.

  • Do not ask others to deliver messages for you while muted or banned.
  • Use the official appeal process if you believe a punishment was issued incorrectly.
  • Repeated attempts to evade moderation may result in a longer suspension or permanent ban.
Enforcement: Depending on severity, evidence, and prior history, staff may issue a warning, remove content, mute, temporarily suspend, or permanently ban an account. Serious violations may result in immediate action. Staff should consider the circumstances and available evidence.
